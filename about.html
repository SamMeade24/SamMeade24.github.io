About Me

My Journey

Hi, I’m Sam. After over a decade working in digital agencies, helping brands grow and managing client campaigns, I became increasingly interested in the code behind the solutions I was delivering.


That curiosity led me to full-stack development, where I now spend my days learning, experimenting, and building projects that challenge me to think creatively and technically. Every project is an opportunity to sharpen my skills and explore new technologies.


MadeByMeade is where I document my work, the skills I’m developing, and my journey as I grow as a developer.


Along the way, I aim to combine my commercial experience with my technical skills. I’m focused on building projects that are reliable, practical, and improve my understanding of how real-world applications are put together. This page, these projects, and this journey are all part of that process.

My Focus

  • Sharpen my full-stack skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, and Git.
  • Build and maintain projects that reflect my growth and curiosity.
  • Apply what I’m learning to real-world projects and practical use cases.
  • Connect with other developers, share knowledge, and grow my network.
