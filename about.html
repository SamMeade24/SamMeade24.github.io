About Me

My Journey

Hi, I’m Sam. After over a decade working in digital agencies, helping brands grow and navigating the fast-paced world of client campaigns, I realized my curiosity was pulling me toward the code behind the solutions I was delivering.


That curiosity led me to full-stack development, where I now spend my days learning, experimenting, and building projects that challenge me to think creatively and technically. Every project is an opportunity to sharpen my skills and explore new technologies.


MadeByMeade is my personal brand — a signature for the work I build, the skills I’m developing, and the journey I’m sharing as I grow as a developer. Whether it’s a small JavaScript program or a responsive website, each project is crafted with care and curiosity.


Along the way, I aim to combine my commercial experience with my technical skills. I want to build digital experiences that don’t just look good — they work reliably, solve problems, and are fun to interact with. This page, these projects, and this journey are all part of that process.

My Focus

  • Sharpen my full-stack skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, and Git.
  • Build and maintain projects that reflect my growth and curiosity.
  • Take on freelance opportunities that let me apply what I’m learning in real-world scenarios.
  • Connect with other developers, share knowledge, and grow my network.
